# Nazca pampa — exploration music, early sketch (2026-11-03)

Replaces the Nazca exploration v2 direction (slow D pentatonic, harmonics, breath pad), which Sam did not like.
Andean and Afro-Peruvian instead: pan pipes and quena over a rumba strum, cajón and bombo under it.
108 BPM, 4/4, 36 bars = 80 s, E minor (pentatonic in the pipes), chords Em D C Bm two bars each.

Form: intro 1–4 (pampa air, a solo quena call, shaker creeps in) · A 5–12 (the zampoña tune over the groove) ·
B 13–20 (the hummingbird: a running line split between two pipes, sikuri hocket, quena flutters) ·
A2 21–28 (tune again, quena an octave under, charango tremolo in) · C 29–32 (hocket and tune together) · outro 33–36.

Tracks (GM fallbacks in the file):
- Zampoña 1 and 2 (GM 75 pan flute): the two halves of the hocket, panned apart. Breathy, slight late vibrato.
- Quena (GM 73 flute): reedier Andean flute; calls, flutters, the octave-under doubling.
- Nylon guitar (GM 24): rumba-flamenca strum, down on 1 and 3, muted chucks on 2 and 4, up-strokes between.
- Charango (GM 24 in the file; a real charango or a bright mandolin patch): tremolo on the top three chord notes.
- Bass (GM 32 acoustic): root, fifth, root, octave.
- Pampa air (GM 89 pad): quiet root and fifth, more in the intro and outro.
- Percussion, ch 10: bombo 41, cajón bass tone 64, cajón slap 63, ghosts 62, claves 75 (son clave 3-2), shaker 82.

Preview is a synth approximation. Not a prepared loop yet; sections A and B each repeat cleanly for the game once the direction is settled.
