# Pyramid Run — minigame music (draft 1, 2026-11-03)

Source: Music sketches/Egypt atmosphere game.mid (Sam's Logic export). Two bits used, as asked:

1. The arpeggio near the end (Running pulse, bars 25–31): D4 A4 D5 A4 · D#4 A4 D5 A4 · D4 A4 D5 F#5 · F4 A4 D5 A4. The group roots D D# D F are the Searching-hum motif, so the hum is in here without being quoted.
2. The start of the final chord (bar 31): drone D2 D3 A3, then D4, F#4, A4, D5 entering one after another, with the bongo heartbeat under it.

160 BPM, 4/4, bar = 1.5 s. 30 bars = 45 s. Not Glass: no additive process, no cells drifting in and out. It is a chiptune run with a beat.

## Form
- Bars 1–4 INTRO (once): bass pedal D and hats; the arpeggio lights up at bar 3. Drum fill into A.
- Bars 5–12 A, the run: the arpeggio as straight eighths (one group per two beats, cycle every two bars), bass on the group roots with an octave bounce, driving beat (kick 1 / 2-and / 3, snare 2 and 4, open hat on 4-and). Timbale roll at the end of each four bars.
- Bars 13–20 B, the cross-rhythm: the arpeggio in Sam's own dotted-eighth spacing (a 16-note, 3-bar cycle) on the saw lead, four-on-the-floor kick holding 4/4 against it. Bars 19–20 break: bass stabs on D D# D F, timbale roll, fill.
- Bars 21–28 C, the final chord: D4, F#4, A4, D5 stack in over the drone one bar apart, half-time drums, arpeggio at half volume; bars 25–28 everything back in with Sam's bongo heartbeat (his gaps and velocities), fill.
- Bar 29 tail: one D chord hit, so the file plays as a piece.

Loop for the game: bars 5–28 (markers LOOP START / LOOP END are in the file). Intro plays once on Begin.

## Sounds (GM fallbacks in the file; swap in Logic)
- Lead, ch 1 (GM 80 square): the straight arpeggio. Retro Synth square or Alchemy pulse, little or no reverb, slight PWM. The first note of each group is louder.
- Lead 2, ch 2 (GM 81 saw): the dotted-eighth arpeggio in B only. A brighter saw so the cross-rhythm reads as a second voice.
- Bass, ch 3 (GM 38 synth bass): saw with a low-pass around 900 Hz and a sine sub. Short notes.
- Pad, ch 4 (GM 62 synth brass): the final-chord stack. Sam's Glass Harmonium or Breathy Keys from the Logic project would tie it to the atmosphere; slow attack, held.
- Drums, ch 10: kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, crash 49. An 8-bit or tight electronic kit, not an acoustic one.
- Percussion, ch 10: high timbale 65 (Sam's five-hit flam roll) and low bongo 61 (Sam's heartbeat). Studio Percussion from the project is the obvious kit.

The preview mp3 is a plain synth approximation to check the shape, not the sound.
