## The visual language (written before touching anything)

- **Ground.** Night: a near-black brown (#0a0908 to #141110) that never goes blue or pure black. Text is cream (#e8d5b7), emphasis a paler cream, links a warm gold (#d4a574). Nothing on screen is white except the paper.
- **Palette by place.** Each room has a low colour that sits behind the words rather than on them: the French amber, the Colony a sickly green, Copper's cellar blue-grey, Trisha's rouge (and her neon frame, the one deliberately loud thing), Ronnie's smoky purple, Cecil Court parchment amber, the Coach pub amber. Each dream world has a colour that drifts behind the page: mountain blue, pampa ochre, island turquoise, pyramid gold, Chebar violet, sanctum bone. Lily's colour is lilac (the Interval's pane, the polaroid's halo). The street is sodium amber high and to the right, wet-pavement blue at the foot.
- **Type.** Crimson Text for prose; Playfair Display for titles, signposted choices and small-caps wayfinding (NOTEBOOK, EXIT TO THE STREET); a typewriter face on paper (the Night Ahead, the phone calls, the polaroid page); Courier for the captions and ENTER buttons of the 3D scenes. Letterspaced capitals mean "a sign", never a heading.
- **Texture.** Paper, ink, brass rules and small ornaments, the lily-of-the-valley emblem ("always take a flower"), the lamp. Panels are dark glass with a hairline, not boxes.
- **Motion.** Slow. Breaths and drifts of 8 to 26 seconds; passages fade in over half a second; nothing snaps. Under reduced motion everything holds still.
- **Ghosts and traces (Sam's rule: beauty here is spectral, the trace, the ghost, the memory).** Titles carry after-images of themselves; the passage just left stays on the eye for under a second; the notebook's map shows through its paper; footprints fade on the wet map; ink prints through the memory photographs; the lamps lie on the wet pavement; the Third Pillar's gold lies in the water. A trace is always faint, warm or cold, and leaving.

## Touch 1 — the colour of the room just left (18:30–18:55 UTC) — committed
UserScript "THE COLOUR OF THE ROOM JUST LEFT" plus `.dss-afterglow` in the stylesheet. When a passage carrying a room's or a world's colour (the seven venues, Lackland's, the five dream worlds, the sanctum and synthesis, Dido's pyre, the Green Sea) is left for one that does not carry the same colour, a fixed radial wash of that colour, screen-blended, arrives with the new page at 32% and fades over five and a half seconds after a 0.7s hold (so it is full while the new passage fades in). Opacity only, no movement, so it stays under reduced motion. `window.DSS_AFTERGLOW = false` switches it off; `DSS_AFTERGLOW_ALPHA` retunes it. Colours are the ones the passage backgrounds and dream tints already use, so nothing new enters the palette. Placement proved with the colour forced to alpha 1 (`beauty1-afterglow-colony-loud-proof.png`), then set quiet. Before/after `beauty1-afterglow-colony-{before,after-0s,after-2s}.png`; phone `beauty1-afterglow-trishas-phone.png` (Trisha's rouge on the street). First tint at 22% with no hold was invisible at play size; 32% with the hold reads as a cast on the arriving page that is gone by the time you have read the first line.
