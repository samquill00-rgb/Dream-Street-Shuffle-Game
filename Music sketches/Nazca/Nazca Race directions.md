# Nazca Race — minigame music, sketch 2 (2026-11-03, on Sam's revised tune)

The pampa tune and the hummingbird hocket from the Nazca sketches, driven like Springsteen: straight eighths everywhere,
piano pumping the chords, organ held under, overdriven guitar chugging power chords, electric bass on eighths, backbeat with
tambourine, a glockenspiel doubling the tune an octave up, and a tenor sax taking the chorus hook. The pan pipes stay in it:
they carry the verse tune and the hocket, so the Andean thread runs through the race.

138 BPM, 4/4, 46 bars = 80 s. Verse stays on Em D C Bm (the pampa); the chorus opens out to G D Em C.

Form: intro 1–4 (the engine turns over: bass and guitar, half-time drums, tune pickup) · verse 5–12 (zampoña tune with glock) ·
chorus 13–20 (sax hook) · verse 2 21–28 (hocket over the drive, sax answers) · chorus 2 29–36 (pipes and sax together) ·
break 37–40 (floor toms and bass, hocket alone) · build 41–44 (C C D D, pipes climbing) · bar 45 one hit.
Loop for the game: bars 5–44 (markers LOOP START / LOOP END in the file); intro once on the start.
