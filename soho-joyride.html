Building Soho.

HANDOFF NOTES

Art pass over the Soho joyride blockout (kept beside this file as soho-joyride-blockout.html). The route still follows the game's compressed map: junctions and stops hold the same tile coordinates and the same arrival payloads, and the same choices are offered at Frith Street and at Old Compton Street. The scene is now three.js (the copy in vendor/three), one world tile to five metres.

The blockout drew Soho mirror-imaged, so a left turn swung right on screen. Here the direction word on each button is worked out from the car's heading (turnWord), and window.__joyride.log records each turn the car actually makes.

buildCity lays the streets out from STREET and SPECIALS (the French, Ronnie Scott's, the Colony's door, Lackland's window, the phone box, the Ginger Light's lamp and the alleys, each where the walkable map has it). buildCar makes the cabin, the driver and the outside of the car. Shop signs other than the two venues are set dressing. The south-west corner of Bateman Street and Frith Street is a lit all-night counter after Hopper's Nighthawks (hawkN, hawkE), built from this file's own boxes with no lettering on its fascia. There is no rain: the road is wet, the glass is dry (Sam, 20 Nov: "dry windscreen"). The car is left-hand drive (LHD): the cabin is modelled right-hand drive and mirrored, with the lettered dials flipped back. A moment after you get in the driver reaches over and puts on the stereo: roger-that.mp3, Sam's own "Roger that!", looped; it fades as the car leaves. ?mute=1 silences it.

For integration, start the ride from a hub encounter link. On exit the page emits dss:joyride-exit on document with the stop's arrival as its detail; set window.dssSohoArrival to that before returning to Dean Street. Cancel the frame loop when leaving the ride (window.__joyride.stop()). Check the Bateman Street position against the live map if the tiles change. The driver is a shape in the dark and says nothing: his identity, the story prose and the dialogue are Sam's.

Preview aids, not for the game: the corner map, the payload line, ?speed=4 to run the clock faster, moving the pointer to look around.
