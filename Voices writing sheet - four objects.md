# Inner voices: four objects

Write your line after each `>`. Leave a `>` empty and that voice stays quiet on that object. When it's filled in, `scratchpad/inner-voices-2026-10-07/sheet_apply.py` puts the lines into the game.

- **THE PRIME**: you now.
- **THE OLDBOY**: the idealised you (the child, the imagined future).
- **THE THIRST**: speaks only when sobriety or morale is under 30, or in the small hours.
