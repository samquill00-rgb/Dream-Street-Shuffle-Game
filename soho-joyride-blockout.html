Handoff notes

The route follows the game’s compressed map. At Frith Street and Old Compton Street the player chooses which road the driver takes. The frontages and cabin are stylised reference geometry for the art pass. Use the game’s visual references for the venue façades and material palette.

In the script, junctions, blocks and venues hold the layout. drawStreet and drawCabin are independent render passes. SOHO_VENUE marks the only venue signs in the blockout. The entry point is a placeholder kerbside encounter on Bateman Street. Side-street directions are prototype UI labels.

For integration, start the ride from a hub encounter link. Carry over lookAt and the arrival payload from stops. In the game's page lifecycle, cancel the animation when leaving the ride. On exit set window.dssSohoArrival before returning to Dean Street. The demo emits dss:joyride-exit with that payload. Check the Bateman test position against the live map if the tiles change.
