Sound effects board

Every cue in the game, each on a button, played by the game's own audio engine. Green buttons are the ones added on the night of 18 October. Room ticks are what a room does by itself every 8 to 30 seconds; close-ups are what you hear when you reach for a thing in a 3D room; the rest fire from the story. The game's own mute setting is left exactly as it was.
